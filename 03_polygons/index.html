#30DayMapChallenge · Day 3: Polygons

The Earth's surface cut into the flat polygon faces of polyhedra, ordered by number of faces. Each face shows the part of the globe behind it as seen from the Earth's centre (gnomonic projection): great circles stay straight, and the map stretches towards the corners. The more faces, the closer the solid gets to the sphere.

Drag to rotate all of them together.
