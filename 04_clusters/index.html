#30DayMapChallenge · Day 4: Clusters

Every bar, school or church in mainland France and Corsica, taken from OpenStreetMap and grouped into clusters that split as you zoom in. Around zoom 10 a cluster is roughly one town. Click a cluster to zoom in. Bars gather in cities, while churches are spread across every village.
